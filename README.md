Transcriptomic datasets related to AF (GSE79768 and GSE115574) and HF (GSE17800 and GSE141910) were retrieved from the GEO database. Differentially expressed genes were identified, and weighted gene co-expression network analysis was employed to determine key modules positively correlated with each disease. Shared genes were identified through intersecting AF- and HF-associated modules. Combinatorial diagnostic models were constructed to evaluate the diagnostic performance of these shared genes. The expression of key genes was validated using RT-qPCR and Western blot assays. In addition, single-cell RNA sequencing datasets (GSE224997 and GSE226314) were integrated to analyze immune cell infiltration patterns in AF and HF
